SovereignWeb.site CRM

Many small CRMs, each in its own encrypted vault

Companies, people, every action taken with them, and what needs you today. It runs in your browser and opens your vault with your own keys: this site never sees your contacts.

Try the demo Learn it in fourteen steps

No sign-up. The demo is made up, at reserved example domains; nothing you do there is written anywhere.

What it does

  • Today

    One line per company that needs you: overdue first, then no reply for 5 days, then drafts to review. One button each.

    Watch how (Today)→

  • Follow-ups

    A company comes back to you 5 days after your last email, until they answer; before you nudge again, it tells you when you last did.

    Watch how (Follow-ups)→

  • Outbox, signed by you

    Write an email on a company page and sign it off with your own key; the Email Cell sends only what you signed.

    Watch how (Outbox, signed by you)→

  • Many small CRMs

    A personal one, one per team, one per project: each its own encrypted vault, with its own keys.

    Watch how (Many small CRMs)→

  • QR key hand-over

    Log in with your passkey, send your QR code, drop the one you get back: the vault opens, in your browser only.

    Watch how (QR key hand-over)→

  • Verified keys

    Add the key a contact gave you, checked and signed for with yours. Every view checks it again.

    Watch how (Verified keys)→

  • For you

    Questions from the agents, sealed to your key, answered on the site with a signature, not lost in a chat.

    Watch how (For you)→

  • Import a spreadsheet

    Paste a CSV: companies matched by domain, people by email, nothing guessed, shown before it is written.

    Watch how (Import a spreadsheet)→

Your contacts stay yours

  • An encrypted vault per CRM

    Every company, person, action and key is a file in a small sgit vault. The vault service holds only encrypted bytes.

  • Your keys, in your browser

    Your browser makes its own key pair, behind your passkey. Its private half cannot leave the browser, not even to this page.

  • A static site, nothing else

    No server code, no cookies, no tracking, no database. Every change is shown to you before it is written.
