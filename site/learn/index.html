Learn the CRM

Thirteen steps, in order: a short presentation, ten explainers, the guide, and then the CRM itself. Each one ends with a link to the next.

  1. The CRM in 13 slides Presentation
    What the CRM is, what it does and how it keeps your contacts in your own vault.
  2. The CRM, and its demo Video
    A walk through the demo: Today, the lead board, a company and its people.
  3. How to use the CRM Guide
    Step by step: the demo, logging in, getting a vault, adding people, logging actions.
  4. Logging in, and the QR key hand-over Video
    Log in on You, send your QR code, and drop the one you get back.
  5. Today, and logging an action Video
    An overdue company, a follow-up logged, and the next step set.
  6. Follow-ups Video
    The 5-day rule: what puts a company on the list and what takes it off.
  7. Writing an email, and signing it off Video
    Write an email from a company page and sign it off with your key, for the Email Cell to send.
  8. Importing a spreadsheet Video
    Bring your contacts in from a CSV file: what is matched, what is skipped.
  9. Verified keys on people Video
    Add a contact's key from the QR code they sent, checked and signed for with yours.
  10. How the agents build the CRM in parallel Video
    Who builds what, in which repository, and how work moves between the agents.
  11. Asking the CRM's agent for something Video
    Send a request from the Requests page, signed with your key, and read the answer.
  12. For you: questions from the agents Video
    A question sealed to your key, answered on the site and signed with it.
  13. Open the CRM The app
    Start in the demo, then log in on You.
