For you: questions from the agents

This explainer plays in your browser with JavaScript. Its script is below.

Script (the captions, in order)
  1. When an agent needs you to decide something, it seals the question to your browser's key.
  2. Today says so: 1 decision for you.
  3. For you lists each question. It was sealed to this browser's key by the agent that asks, so only this browser can open it.
  4. It says who asked and by when, and that their signature was checked.
  5. Pick one option in each group. Here, a made-up question: which view should open first, and in which order.
  6. Here: Today, overdue first. The recommendation is there too, with its evidence.
  7. Sign and send my answer signs it with your key and sends it back through the Requests lane. In this explainer nothing is sent.
  8. The question is sealed to you and the answer signed by you. That is the CRM: open it, and start on Today.

Source: script.json (what the capture does) · scenes.json (what the player shows) · manifest.json (hashes).
