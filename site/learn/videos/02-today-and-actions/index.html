Today, and logging an action

This explainer plays in your browser with JavaScript. Its script is below.

Script (the captions, in order)
  1. The daily loop: see what is due, do it, log it, set the next step.
  2. Start every day on Today. It lists what to do now, each with its action.
  3. First, overdue. Example Ventures asked questions on our proposal, and the answer was due on 21 November.
  4. Say we have answered them by email. Press Log the follow-up on its card.
  5. The company's page opens on Log an action: what, which way, when, who, and one line on what happened.
  6. Here: an email, we reached out, on 23 November, to Diogo Quinn, answering their questions.
  7. Show what will be written shows the exact file first. In the demo it stays in this tab: nothing is written anywhere.
  8. Press Write it. The action is now first in the company's Activity. In a real vault, it is one change you can see in its history.
  9. Now the next step. Edit stage, owner and next step keeps the lead row up to date.
  10. The next step becomes: wait for their decision, due 30 November.
  11. Show what will be written, then Write it. The company now shows the new next step.
  12. Back on Today, Example Ventures is no longer overdue. Do this for each card, every day.
  13. Next: follow-ups.

Source: script.json (what the capture does) · scenes.json (what the player shows) · manifest.json (hashes).
