Logging in, and the QR key hand-over

This explainer plays in your browser with JavaScript. Its script is below.

Script (the captions, in order)
  1. Your browser makes its own key. You send its public half as a QR code, and get a vault back the same way.
  2. Press You, type your name as the team knows you, and press Log in. Log in on the device you will use.
  3. Here, a made-up name: Sam Sample.
  4. Your browser makes its own key. Its private half never leaves this browser and cannot be copied out. Its fingerprint is hidden here.
  5. On the left, 1. Send this: your key as a QR code, already copied for you (hidden here). Send it to Dinis on WhatsApp. It is public: it only lets someone seal things that this browser alone can open.
  6. On the right, 2. Drop what you get back: when Dinis sends a QR code back, drop the picture there, or paste it anywhere on the page.
  7. The page checks the code was signed by the CRM's agent and opens it. The vault then appears here, under Your vaults. Press Open.
  8. The top right always says which vault is open. Press it to switch, or to go back to the demo.
  9. On a shared or borrowed computer, use Log out of this browser when you finish.
  10. A vault's key opens everything in it. Share it only through the sealed codes, never in a chat or an email.
