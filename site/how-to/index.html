How to use the CRM

The CRM keeps every company, every person and every action the team takes with them in one place, so nobody falls through the gaps. It runs in your browser. Your contacts live in an encrypted vault that only people with its key can open; this site never sees them.

Open the CRM

1. Try the demo first

The CRM opens on Today, in demo mode: every company, person and email in it is made up. The top right says "Vault: Demo" and a banner says so. Click around freely: nothing you do in the demo is saved anywhere.

  • Today: what to do now, overdue first, then follow-ups and drafts waiting, each with its action.
  • Lead board: one row per company, with its stage, owner, draft, last contact and next step.
  • Follow-ups: everyone we reached out to who has not replied in 5 days or more.
  • Contacts: everyone, with their company and when we were last in touch.

2. Log in

  1. Press You, type your name as the team knows you, and press Log in.
  2. Your browser makes its own key. Its private half never leaves this browser and cannot be copied out. If your device has a passkey (Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello), it asks you to make one for this site, and asks for it again before it opens a vault.
  3. The page is in two halves. On the left, 1. Send this: your key as a QR code, already copied for you. Paste it into WhatsApp to Dinis. It is public: it only lets someone seal things that this browser alone can open.
  4. On the right, 2. Drop what you get back: when Dinis sends a QR code back, drop the picture there, or paste it anywhere on the page.

Log in on the device you will use. A key made in one browser stays in that browser.

3. Get a vault

A vault is one CRM: a team's, or your own. You can hold several.

  • A team vault you are given: send your QR code (step 2 above) and drop the QR code you get back. The page checks it was signed by the CRM's agent, opens it, and the vault appears under Your vaults. Press Open.
  • Your own personal vault: on Open your vault, under Start a new CRM vault, give your name and your SG/Send access token and press Create my CRM vault. The page shows the vault's key once: save it in your password manager before you go on.

The top right always says which vault is open ("Vault: Demo", or the vault's name); press it to switch. Back to the demo (on Open your vault) closes a vault in that tab.

4. Add companies and people

  1. On the lead board, open Add a company: name, domain, kind and the next step.
  2. On a company's page, open Add a person: name, email and role.
  3. Press Show what will be written. The page shows exactly the files it will add. Only Write it writes them to the vault, as one change you can see in its history.

Writing needs your SG/Send access token. The page asks for it when it needs it.

5. Log every action

On a company's page, Log an action records each thing done with them: an email, a LinkedIn message, a call, a meeting, a message or a note; whether we reached out or they replied; the date; who; and one line on what happened. It appears in the company's Activity with its emails. Edit stage, owner and next step keeps the lead row up to date.

Anything where we reached out last and had no reply for 5 days shows on Follow-ups. Check it every day.

6. Import a spreadsheet

  1. On Import, download the template.
  2. Fill it in, one line per person (or per company). Only the company is required. Empty cells stay empty: nothing is guessed.
  3. Paste it, or choose the file, and press Show what will be written. The page says how many companies, people and contacts it will add, and lists any line it cannot use and why.
  4. Press Write it. Companies already there are matched by domain or name, people by email, and nothing that is already there is changed.

Enter only people you have a reason to keep in touch with, and only what they shared with you.

7. Ask the CRM's agent for something

On Requests, logged in, write what you would like: a feature, a fix, a question or a workflow. It is signed with your browser's key and sealed so that only the agent that builds this CRM can read it. Its answer comes back on the same page, readable only in your browser. Do not put contacts' details in a request.

Keeping it safe

  • A vault's key opens everything in it. Keep it in a password manager and share it only through the sealed codes above, never in a chat or an email.
  • On a shared or borrowed computer, use Log out of this browser on You when you finish.
  • The site keeps no data and sets no cookies. What you see comes from your vault, opened in your browser.
